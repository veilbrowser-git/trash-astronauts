# Picking things up

For the README (three sentences):

Salvage lying on the floor no longer needs a crouch: point an open, empty hand at an item up to about 1.2 m away (it glows and a faint line runs to it from your palm), squeeze, and it travels into your palm in a fraction of a second, heavy things more slowly, and nothing comes through a wall, out of someone else's hand or while you hold a tool. The direct grab is more forgiving too: it reaches a little past the fingertips and under the palm, and when it touches several things it takes the one nearest the palm. In the Moon cave, salvage now stops against the fallen rocks along the walls instead of sliding inside them where no hand could find it.
