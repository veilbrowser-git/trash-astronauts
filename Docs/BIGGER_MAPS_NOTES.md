# Bigger maps

All four wrecks are now 1.5 times longer and wider than before (2.25 times the floor area) measured from the airlock, with ceilings up to 3.4 m (5 m in the barge's ore hold), while doors, buttons, cut panels, handrails and lockers stay their real size and the tug is unchanged.
Every wreck has more lamps, more creature waypoints on the same patrol routes, its loot spots spread out to the far corners, and the freighter is now built at full size instead of being stretched, so its doors and rails are no longer distorted.
To make up for the longer trips, the oxygen tank now holds 13:20 instead of 10:00, and its upgrade adds 4:00 instead of 3:00.
