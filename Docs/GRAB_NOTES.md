# Everyone grabs (co-op stage 3) - text for the README

Three sentences to replace the "only the host can carry salvage (the others see `HOST ONLY` ...)" line in the
README's co-op section once the session adapter forwards the item messages (README.md was not edited here):

Every crew member can now grab, carry, hand over, throw and deposit salvage, and the item is in your hand the moment you close it, without waiting for the host.
If two of you reach for the same item at once, whoever reached the host first gets it, and the other hand gives two short buzzes and comes away empty.
Nobody can take an item out of someone else's hand, but once they let go or throw it anyone can grab or catch it, and the host still decides every deposit and trick shot.
