# Co-op revive (for the README)

In a co-op crew (same Wi-Fi or online), a player the spider catches still gets the full jumpscare, but afterwards they are DOWNED where they were caught instead of the contract failing, as long as someone else in the crew is still up; alone, or if nobody else is up, getting caught fails the contract as before (single-player is unchanged).
A downed player cannot move, grab, use tools or press buttons, but can look around, talk and listen, and their suit shows a glowing beacon on the chest that the crew can find in the dark; the spider leaves them alone and wanders off.
To revive them, hold a hand closed on that chest beacon for about 5 seconds (the ring on the beacon and on both wrists shows how far it is; letting go drains it, and the spider can hear you working).
Every down costs that player 10% of their own pay for the contract (at most 40%), and anyone still down when the crew extracts is brought home by the company for a further 35%; oxygen running out still fails everyone.
